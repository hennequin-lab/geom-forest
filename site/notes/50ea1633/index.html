let x = Cga.point3 1. 2. 3. in

Nx.to_array (Cga.coords x)                  (* [|1.; 2.; 3.|] *)

The quadratic term is the whole trick. It is what makes the inner product of two points depend only on their separation (Distance is an inner product), and it is why the representation is nonlinear in the coordinates while every operation on it is linear in the algebra.
