The outer product spans subspaces

#ga/foundation

The subscript means "keep only the grade- part", so the sum collects exactly the terms whose grades add to . The product is therefore associative, bilinear, and antisymmetric on vectors: and . So is not "the area"; it is the oriented parallelogram spanned by and , with the area as its magnitude. A wedge of independent vectors is a grade- blade, the subspace they span. Dependence is visible algebraically: if the vectors are linearly dependent, their wedge is zero.
