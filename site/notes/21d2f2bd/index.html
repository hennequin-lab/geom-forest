One functor and three models, plus a packed motion type.

The interfaces are the documentation: each .mli fixes the sign conventions and points at the section of geom/doc/notes.typ that derives them. When in doubt about a sign, read the .mli; when in doubt about a normalisation, read Numerical cautions in a projective algebra.

The library is batched throughout (The last axis is the blade axis) and traceable throughout (Traced algebra: no host branches), so there is no separate "fast path" API. The one place where you choose a representation is motion: packed A motor is a dual quaternion or dense embedded.
