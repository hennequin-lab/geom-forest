A dilation is a boost in the e4e5 plane

#ga/model

A dilation by the factor is generated by , which is the bivector mixing the two extra dimensions:

It is a hyperbolic versor, the second case of the bivector exponential. It fixes the origin and rescales the two null directions: and . Its sandwich action on a point is projective: the raw image has coefficient , so Cga.coords divides by that and the Euclidean coordinates are multiplied by .

open Geom

let d = Cga.dilator (Nx.scalar Nx.float32 0.5) in
let x = Cga.point3 1. 0. 0. in
Nx.to_array (Cga.coords (Cga.sandwich d x))   (* e^0.5 * [|1.; 0.; 0.|] *)

Two uses in robotics. A scale-normalised objective can be made scale-invariant by composing with a dilation, and a workspace defined up to similarity can be canonicalised with one. More importantly, the dilation is the simplest example of the projective character of the whole conformal model: the versor action is linear on the null cone, but the induced action on coordinates is only defined up to scale, and something has to fix the scale (Numerical cautions in a projective algebra).
