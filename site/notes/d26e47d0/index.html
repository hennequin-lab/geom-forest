A signature counts positive, negative and null directions

#ga/foundation

The signature is the diagonal of the metric in an orthonormal basis: a list of , and entries, counted as . It is the only datum the algebra needs beyond the dimension, and it decides which geometry you get.

Three cases earn names in this forest:

  • , written Cl(3,0,0): three Euclidean directions, the rotation algebra E3.
  • , written Cl(3,0,1): three Euclidean directions and one null direction, plane-based projective geometry PGA.
  • , written Cl(4,1): four positive and one negative direction, the conformal model CGA.
