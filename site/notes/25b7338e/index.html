The commutator makes bivectors a Lie algebra

#ga/foundation

The commutator product is half the antisymmetric part of the geometric product:

On bivectors it is the Lie bracket of the Lie algebra , and it measures how two infinitesimal rotations fail to commute. The The even subalgebra holds the rotations's bivectors are the Lie algebra of the spin group, so the commutator is the algebraic source of every structure you would otherwise import from Lie theory: generators of motion, the adjoint action and the Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff expansion behind the exponential.

open Geom

let b12 = E3.op E3.e1 E3.e2 in
let b23 = E3.op E3.e2 E3.e3 in
E3.to_array (E3.commutator b12 b23)   (* -e13: the third rotation plane *)

In the conformal model the six bivectors and close under the commutator into the Lie algebra of SE(3); adding and the three gives the ten-dimensional conformal algebra. That is the whole reason a motor can carry a Lie-group structure while looking like a multivector.

The library exposes it as Algebra.commutator, with no claim beyond the bivector case.
