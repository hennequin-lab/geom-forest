A motor is a dual quaternion

#ga/motion

A rigid motion is a rotation followed by a translation, , and it is an even unit A versor is an invertible product of vectors of the subalgebra generated by the Euclidean bivectors and the mixed bivectors . That subalgebra is eight-dimensional, with basis

and it is isomorphic to the dual quaternions. This is the most important implementation fact in the forest: a pose is eight numbers, not 32. The dense even subalgebra of Cl(4,1) is sixteen-dimensional, and the rigid part is half of it.

open Geom
let v3 x y z = Nx.create Nx.float32 [| 3 |] [| x; y; z |] in
let sc x = Nx.scalar Nx.float32 x in

let m =
  Motor.mul (Motor.translate3 0. 0. 1.)
    (Motor.rotate ~axis:(v3 0. 0. 1.) ~angle:(sc (Float.pi /. 2.)))
in
Nx.to_array (Motor.translation m)            (* [|0.; 0.; 1.|] *)
Nx.to_array (Cga.coords (Cga.apply_motor m (Cga.point3 1. 0. 0.)))
(* [|0.; 1.; 1.|] *)
