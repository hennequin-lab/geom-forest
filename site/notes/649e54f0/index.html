A transversion bends space without moving infinity

#ga/model

A transversion with parameter vector is generated by the null bivector . The bivector is nilpotent, so the exponential terminates:

The normalised action is the special conformal transformation

which is nonlinear in the coordinates but linear on the null cone. That is the whole point of the embedding: the conformal group acts by linear maps on .

open Geom
let v3 x y z = Nx.create Nx.float32 [| 3 |] [| x; y; z |] in

let k = Cga.transversion (v3 0.5 0. 0.) in
let x = Cga.point3 1. 0. 0. in
Nx.to_array (Cga.coords (Cga.sandwich k x))   (* [|0.6667; 0.; 0.|] *)

A transversion is not a rigid motion, so it has no Motor.t representation; it lives only in the dense algebra (One motor algebra, two models). That is why the renderer's Primitive.transform can only apply motors, and why conformal transformations of a scene must be applied to the algebra objects and re-extracted (Adapters from algebra objects to primitives).
