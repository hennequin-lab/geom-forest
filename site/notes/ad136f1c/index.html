A blade is a subspace with a magnitude

#ga/foundation

The grade of a blade is the number of basis vectors in the product, so it is also the dimension of the subspace the blade represents. A grade-1 blade is a vector: a line through the origin, with a length. A grade-2 blade is a bivector: an oriented plane, with an area. A grade-3 blade is a volume. In dimensions the grades run from (scalars) to (the The pseudoscalar is the whole space as one blade).
