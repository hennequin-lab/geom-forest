The left contraction is the metric projection

#ga/foundation

The left contraction is the grade-lowering part of the geometric product:

On vectors it is the ordinary dot product, so it carries the metric. On a vector and a bivector it does something more useful than a number: . Contracting with projects onto the plane and rotates by a quarter turn inside it. That is the geometric content of "multiply by " in complex numbers, promoted to any dimension.

open Geom

E3.to_array (E3.inner E3.e1 (E3.op E3.e1 E3.e2))  (* e2 *)
E3.to_array (E3.inner E3.e2 (E3.op E3.e1 E3.e2))  (* -e1 *)
E3.to_array (E3.inner E3.e3 (E3.op E3.e1 E3.e2))  (* zero: orthogonal *)

The contraction is what makes incidence a scalar equation in the conformal model. A point lies on a sphere , a plane , a line or a circle exactly when (Distance is an inner product, Round versus flat: the e∞ contraction decides). It is also the natural way to extract the geometric content of an object blade: for a circle is its carrier plane (The carrier plane of a circle).

The library calls it inner, and scalar_prod is its grade-zero companion.
