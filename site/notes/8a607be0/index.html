Three involutions: reverse, grade flip, conjugation

#ga/foundation

Three sign patterns on grade- blades do all the bookkeeping:

They are Reverse and the magnitude of a blade, grade involution and Clifford conjugation. Each is an involution, each is determined by the grade, and each has one job.

  • (grade involution) separates even from odd, so it is the projector behind the even subalgebra.
  • (reverse) is the anti-automorphism that defines the magnitude and the inverse (Invert a blade with its reverse).
  • is the Clifford conjugate, the sign pattern . On motors it is the group inverse; on a quaternion it is the usual conjugate. 
open Geom

let b = E3.op E3.e1 E3.e2 in
Nx.item [] (E3.scalar_prod b (E3.reverse b))          (* 1. *)
Nx.item [] (E3.scalar_prod b (E3.grade_involution b)) (* -1. *)

The three compose: conjugation is reverse after grade involution, and any two of them give the third up to the parity of the grade. The library exposes all three (reverse, grade_involution, conjugate) plus the Blade.*_sign helpers that the precomputed tables are built from.
