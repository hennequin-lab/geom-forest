The multiplication table is 2^n × 2^n signs

#ga/computing

For an orthonormal basis, the product of two basis blades is determined by three integers (Basis blades are bitmasks):

where is the sign of the reordering, is the product of the over the common indices, and the result index is the xor. So the whole algebra is a table of signed indices, computed once at module initialisation. For Cl(4,1) that is entries.

open Geom

Array.length (Blade.all 5)                            (* 32 blades *)
Blade.mul_sign (Blade.of_basis 1) (Blade.of_basis 2)  (* 1 *)
Blade.mul_sign (Blade.of_basis 2) (Blade.of_basis 1)  (* -1 *)
Blade.to_int (Blade.of_basis 1)                       (* 1 *)

The geometric product of two multivectors is then a batched contraction over the last axis: gather the coefficients of the pairs that land on each output blade, multiply by the table entry, and sum. The implementation can use an explicit structure tensor in a single einsum, or the equivalent gather-and-sum with the xor table; both are traceable, and the choice is a performance question rather than a semantic one.

Blade owns the table-free part (indices, grades, signs, complements) and knows nothing about the metric, which is why it can be shared by all three models (Three models, one core).
