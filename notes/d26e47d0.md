The signature is the diagonal of the metric in an orthonormal basis: a list of
$+1$, $-1$ and $0$ entries, counted as $(p, q, r)$. Here $p$ is the number of
positive directions, $q$ the number of negative ones, and $r$ the number of
null ones. The total dimension is $n = p + q + r$. The signature is the only
datum the algebra needs beyond the dimension, and it decides which geometry you
get.

In the orthonormal basis $e_1, dots, e_n$, the [[3122fc58|Clifford relation]] reads

```typst
e_i e_j + e_j e_i = 2 g_(i j), quad g = "diag"(sigma_1, dots, sigma_n),
```

where $g_(i j)$ is the entry in row $i$, column $j$ of the metric matrix, and
$sigma_i in {+1, -1, 0}$ is the square of $e_i$. Off the diagonal the entries
vanish, so for $i != j$ the equation says the basis vectors anticommute, and
for $i = j$ it says $e_i^2 = sigma_i$.

Three cases earn names in this forest:

- $(3,0,0)$, written `Cl(3,0,0)`: three Euclidean directions, the rotation
  algebra [[191286d7|E3]].
- $(3,0,1)$, written `Cl(3,0,1)`: three Euclidean directions and one null
  direction, plane-based projective geometry [[d9428abe|PGA]].
- $(4,1)$, written `Cl(4,1)`: four positive and one negative direction, the
  conformal model [[93d1da38|CGA]]. The negative direction is what makes the two
  null vectors $e_0$ and $e_oo$ possible ([[50ea1633|A conformal point is a null vector]]).

A null direction ($sigma_i = 0$) is not a degeneracy to be avoided: it is what
makes [[ccd5c487|the projective model]] projective. It also breaks the Hodge
star, because the [[afad7126|The pseudoscalar is the whole space as one blade]] is no longer invertible; that is the whole
reason [[b5dd2ed2|In a degenerate algebra the dual becomes the complement]] exists.

```ocaml
open Geom

let s = Signature.conformal3 in
Signature.to_string s                       (* "(4,1,0)" *)
(Signature.dim s, Signature.size s)         (* (5, 32) *)
Signature.pseudoscalar_square s             (* -1 *)
Signature.pseudoscalar_square (Signature.projective 3)  (* 0 *)
```

`Signature.make` rejects any entry that is not $-1$, $0$ or $1$, so an invalid
metric cannot reach the algebra. The models in the library fix the signature
once, at module initialisation, which lets them precompute the whole
[[6c390d07|multiplication table]].
