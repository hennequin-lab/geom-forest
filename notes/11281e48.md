A multivector is an element of the algebra: a formal sum
$A = sum_A A_A e_A$ over the [[9ae37e30|blades]]. In software it is just
the array of its $2^n$ coefficients, in increasing bitmask order. Nothing about
the algebra is hidden in the type; the operations read and write that array.

```ocaml
open Geom

let a = E3.gp E3.e1 E3.e2 in
E3.to_array a                 (* [|0.; 0.; 0.; 1.; 0.; 0.; 0.; 0.|] *)
Blade.to_string (Blade.of_int 3)  (* "e12": index 3 has the coefficient 1 *)
E3.coeff a (Blade.of_int 3)   (* 1. *)
```

`coeff` and `with_coeff` are the accessors that name a blade directly, and
they are the API you want in tests and in debugging. `grade k` keeps one grade
and zeros the rest; `even` and `odd` do it for a whole parity.

The coefficient vector is also the batching axis convention: a multivector has
shape `[|2^n|]`, a batch of $b$ of them has shape `[|b; 2^n|]`, and an image
of points has shape `[|h; w; 2^n|]`. The leading axes are ordinary broadcast
axes for `Nx`, so every algebra operation is one batched contraction over the
last axis. [[095f0641|The last axis is the blade axis]] spells out the consequences; [[7d10658e|Store the orthonormal basis, read the null basis]]
explains why the coefficient basis is the orthonormal one even in the conformal
model.
