A rigid motion is a rotation followed by a translation, $M = T R$, and it is an
even unit [[59a585e7|A versor is an invertible product of vectors]] of the subalgebra generated by the Euclidean bivectors
$e_(i j)$ and the mixed bivectors $e_i e_oo$. That subalgebra is
eight-dimensional, with basis

```typst
{1, e_12, e_13, e_23, e_1 e_oo, e_2 e_oo, e_3 e_oo, e_123 e_oo},
```

and it is isomorphic to the dual quaternions. This is the most important
implementation fact in the forest: a pose is eight numbers, not 32. The dense
even subalgebra of `Cl(4,1)` is sixteen-dimensional, and the rigid part is half
of it.

```ocaml
open Geom
let v3 x y z = Nx.create Nx.float32 [| 3 |] [| x; y; z |] in
let sc x = Nx.scalar Nx.float32 x in

let m =
  Motor.mul (Motor.translate3 0. 0. 1.)
    (Motor.rotate ~axis:(v3 0. 0. 1.) ~angle:(sc (Float.pi /. 2.)))
in
Nx.to_array (Motor.translation m)            (* [|0.; 0.; 1.|] *)
Nx.to_array (Cga.coords (Cga.apply_motor m (Cga.point3 1. 0. 0.)))
(* [|0.; 1.; 1.|] *)
```

`Motor.t` is a plain tensor of shape `batch @ [|8|]`, so it batches, traces and
differentiates like everything else ([[095f0641|The last axis is the blade axis]]). It acts by the
[[dc7fd3f4|The sandwich is an algebra automorphism]] $X |-> M X tilde(M)$, without ever materialising the 32-coefficient
embedding, and that action preserves grades and incidence.

Because the type is shared between the projective and conformal models
([[432aee25|One motor algebra, two models]]), kinematics never has to choose a model. The mathematics is
in [[ad74741e|A translator is a null rotation]] for the translation part, [[bcd06d42|Rotating about an axis through a point]] for the
rotation part, and [[6132d759|The SE(3) exponential turns a twist into a screw]] for the general screw.
