One functor and three models, plus a packed motion type.

| module | what it is | where to read the maths |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Blade` | bitmask blades, grades, signs, complements | [[9ae37e30|Basis blades are bitmasks]] |
| `Signature` | $(p,q,r)$, pseudoscalar square | [[d26e47d0|A signature counts positive, negative and null directions]] |
| `Algebra` | the dense functor: products, involutions, duals, norms, sandwich, adjoint | [[b65e7fa9|The geometric product]], [[d32870e7|The Hodge dual trades a blade for its complement]], [[59a585e7|A versor is an invertible product of vectors]] |
| `Versor` | `exp`, `log`, `exp_simple`, `slerp` for a dense algebra | [[0aa35a84|Exponentiating a simple bivector closes in three cases]] |
| `Motor` | packed 8-component rigid motions, exp/log, slerp | [[146686de|A motor is a dual quaternion]] |
| `E3` | `Cl(3,0,0)`: vectors, rotors | [[191286d7|Cl(3,0,0) is the Euclidean rotation algebra]] |
| `Pga` | `Cl(3,0,1)`: planes, lines, points, meet/join | [[d9428abe|Cl(3,0,1) is plane-based projective geometry]] |
| `Cga` | `Cl(4,1)`: points, spheres, lines, circles, motors | [[93d1da38|Cl(4,1) is the conformal model of 3D space]] |

```ocaml
open Geom
let v3 x y z = Nx.create Nx.float32 [| 3 |] [| x; y; z |] in
let sc x = Nx.scalar Nx.float32 x in

(* the generic surface, shared by the three models *)
E3.gp E3.e1 E3.e2                            (* products *)
Cga.reverse (Cga.plane ~normal:(v3 0. 0. 1.) ~offset:(sc 0.))
(* model-specific surface *)
Cga.incident (Cga.point3 0. 0. 1.)
  (Cga.plane ~normal:(v3 0. 0. 1.) ~offset:(sc 0.))
Motor.mul (Motor.translate3 1. 0. 0.) Motor.one
```

The interfaces are the documentation: each `.mli` fixes the sign conventions and
points at the section of `geom/doc/notes.typ` that derives them. When in doubt
about a sign, read the `.mli`; when in doubt about a normalisation, read
[[1d3a2467|Numerical cautions in a projective algebra]].

The library is batched throughout ([[095f0641|The last axis is the blade axis]]) and traceable throughout
([[863e305f|Traced algebra: no host branches]]), so there is no separate "fast path" API. The one place where you
choose a representation is motion: packed [[146686de|A motor is a dual quaternion]] or dense
[[432aee25|embedded]].
